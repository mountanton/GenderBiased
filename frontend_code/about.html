Hypatia Leaderboard - Gender Bias Evaluation

Comparison and ranking of AI models under the scope of AI ethics, specifically gender bias.

About Our Project & Team

This project benchmarks Large Language Models (LLMs) on AI ethics, specifically measuring gender bias across realistic social, educational, and professional scenarios. The benchmark evaluates models across 10 distinct thematic topics using 656 questions per run. To ensure rigorius evaluation independent of wording, the dataset employs systematic substitutions (varying names, occupations, and caregiving activities) alongside flipped counterparts via gender-swapping to decouple genuine bias from acquiescence (a model's tendency to answer "Yes"). In scenarios with no explicit evidence, an unbiased model is expected to assign roles neutrally at a 50% baseline; deviations above 50% reflect stereotypical bias, while rates below 50% indicate counter-stereotypical tendencies.
